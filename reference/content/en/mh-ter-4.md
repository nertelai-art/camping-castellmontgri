# MH TER 4

**Capacity:** 4

**Zona:** 2

**Air-conditioning:** ✓

**Area:** 26 m²

Location

The MH Ter offers a comfortable and functional accommodation experience for families and small groups. It consists of a room with a double bed (135×190), and a room with two single beds (80×190). There is also a sofa bed available.

The accommodation also includes a full bathroom with shower and WC for added convenience. The kitchen is fully equipped with microwave, spacious refrigerator (145×55), and cooking utensils, perfect for preparing homemade meals during your stay.

These accommodations are designed to provide everything necessary for a comfortable stay and are located in different areas of the campground to suit guests' preferences.

- **Optional extras:** towels (price €7 + €25 refundable deposit per pack) and barbecue
- Additional tents or pets are not allowed.
