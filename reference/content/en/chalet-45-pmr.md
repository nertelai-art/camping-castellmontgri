# CHALET 4/5 PMR

**Capacity:** 5

**Zona:** 2

**Air-conditioning:** ✓

**Area:** 35 m²

Location

The **Chalet 4 PMR** is the version adapted for people with reduced mobility of our most modern and elegant accommodation. They stand out for their spaciousness, sophisticated design and attention to detail. Each chalet has a bright living room with a contemporary design, a spacious dining room and a fully equipped kitchen with gas cooker, dishwasher, microwave and fridge.

With two spacious bedrooms (one with twin beds and one with a double bed), and a full bathroom, these chalets are designed for maximum comfort and functionality.

One of its main attractions is its impressive wooden terrace, which is perfectly integrated with the living room thanks to a large sliding window. This connection creates a continuous space that enhances the feeling of spaciousness and luminosity, ideal for enjoying both indoors and outdoors. The terrace is equipped with table, chairs and an awning, providing the ideal environment for relaxing outdoors.

Extras: towels (price €7 + €25 refundable deposit per pack) and barbecue.
Restrictions: additional tents and animals are not allowed.
