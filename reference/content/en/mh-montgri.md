# MH MONTGRÍ

**Capacity:** 6/8

**Zona:** 3

**Air-conditioning:** ✓

**Area:** 32 m²

Location

The MH Montgrí are an ideal option for those seeking a comfortable and well-equipped stay at the campsite. These accommodations feature a cozy living room, a dining area, and a kitchen equipped with a gas stove and refrigerator. They have three bedrooms: two with single beds and one with a double bed, providing suitable space for families or small groups. The bathroom includes a shower, sink, and toilet, offering all necessary amenities.

Outside, the MH Montgrí have a small terrace with a table and chairs, perfect for enjoying outdoor meals or relaxing moments.

These accommodations are located in the lower part of the campsite, in the yellow zone, which makes access to the campsite facilities and services easy.

- **Available extras:** towels (price €7 + €25 refundable deposit per pack) and barbecue
- No additional tents or pets allowed.
