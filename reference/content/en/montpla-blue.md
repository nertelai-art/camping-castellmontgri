# GLAMPING EMPORDÀ SUPERIOR

**Capacity:** 5

**Zona:** 2

**Air-conditioning:** ✓

**Area:** 35 m²

Location

**Glamping Empordà Superior** is designed to offer an exceptional experience that combines the adventure of camping with comfort. It features a fully equipped kitchen for preparing delicious meals, a sink with shower to freshen up after a day of outdoor activities, and a private toilet for added convenience.

Inside the accommodation, you'll find a cozy double bed for comfortable rest, as well as a room with two single beds, ideal for families or small groups. This cozy and well-equipped environment allows you to enjoy nature without sacrificing modern comforts, making glamping a perfect choice for memorable getaways.

- **Optional extras:** towels (price €7+ €25 refundable deposit per pack) and barbecue
- Additional tents or animals are not allowed
