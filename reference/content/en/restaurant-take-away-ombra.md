# TAKE AWAY OMBRA

**Zona:** Ombra

**Schedule:** 12:00 - 23:00h

Explore an exceptional culinary variety in our Take Away area at Ombra, where you can enjoy a wide range of gastronomic options. From traditional Dutch dishes and exotic flavors of India and Asia to classics like artisan pizzas, burgers, chicken strips, and delicious potatoes. Whatever your craving of the day, our menu is designed to satisfy all tastes quickly and efficiently.
