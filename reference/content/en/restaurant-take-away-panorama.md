# TAKE AWAY PANORAMA

**Zona:** Panorama

**Schedule:** 8:00 - 23:00h

Discover an extraordinary culinary offer in our Take Away area in Panorama. We offer a wide variety of gastronomic options ranging from traditional Dutch dishes and exotic flavors from India and Asia to classic favorites like artisanal pizzas, hamburgers, chicken strips, and delicious fries. Whatever your craving of the day, our menu is designed to satisfy all tastes quickly and efficiently.
