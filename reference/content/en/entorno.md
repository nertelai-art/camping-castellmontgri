## SURROUNDINGS

# GET TO KNOW THE COSTA BRAVA

The environment in which the campsite is located offers a unique experience where nature meets coastal serenity. Surrounded by impressive maritime landscapes and picturesque coves, our campsite immerses you in the beauty of the Mediterranean coast. Enjoy the sea breeze, explore trails that line the coast and discover the rich native flora and fauna. Plus, the proximity to charming coastal towns allows you to immerse yourself in local culture and enjoy fresh gastronomic delights. Whether relaxing by the sea or venturing into the surrounding nature, our campsite on the Costa Brava offers you a unique experience of connecting with the environment.

## Paradisiacal beaches

Discover the serene coastal beauty of the Costa Brava through its paradisiacal beaches and coves. Soak up the sun on the wide golden sand beaches, such as Platja d'Aro, which offer crystal-clear waters and a lively atmosphere. Explore hidden coves, such as Cala Aiguablava, surrounded by lush vegetation, or Cala del Golfet, a quiet corner with turquoise waters.

Every corner of the coast invites you to immerse yourself in peace and natural beauty. Whether relaxing on a dream beach or exploring secluded coves, the Costa Brava gives you a paradisiacal setting to enjoy under the Mediterranean sun.

## Medieval towns

Embark on a journey back in time exploring the charming medieval towns that dot the Costa Brava. Immerse yourself in history as you stroll through cobblestone streets and discover ancient architecture. Tossa de Mar, with its imposing castle and walls, will transport you to bygone eras. Peratallada, with its cobbled streets and stone buildings, invites you to get lost among its corners. Pals, another medieval treasure, will captivate you with its towers and cobbled squares.

These towns, steeped in history, offer an authentic and picturesque experience, enriching your stay on the Costa Brava with their medieval charm.

## Nourished by their culture

Embark on a cultural journey along the Costa Brava, where historical and artistic wealth is intertwined with coastal charm. Immerse yourself in the work of Salvador Dalí by visiting the Dalí Theatre-Museum in Figueres, a unique experience that awakens the imagination. Discover the authenticity of L'Estartit by exploring the fascinating seabed through diving activities, such as the Medes Islands Natural Park. Also, don't miss the opportunity to visit the Mascort Foundation, a space that houses a unique collection of art and photography.

Each place tells a cultural story, offering you an enriching experience that combines tradition with coastal beauty.
