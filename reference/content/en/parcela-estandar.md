# STANDARD PLOT

**Capacity:** 6

**Area:** 72 m²

Location

Standard pitches are perfect for those who enjoy outdoor life and the authentic camping experience. With an area of 70 square meters, each one has access to a water point and electricity with a capacity of 6 amps, ensuring adequate lighting and the ability to charge electrical devices. They are designed to offer comfort and convenience, providing enough space to accommodate up to 6 people, ideal for groups and families.
