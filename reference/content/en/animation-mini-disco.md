# MINI DISCO

**Schedule:** 20:00 - 20:30h

Welcome to our Mini Disco! Every day from 20:00 to 21:00, the little ones enjoy dancing and having fun to the music played by our super DJ. Moreover, both kids and adults have a great time at this event, where sometimes, our mascot Monti makes a surprise appearance to make the children's evening even more joyful at our campsite.
