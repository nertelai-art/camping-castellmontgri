# VILLAGE 4/5

**Capacity:** 4/5

**Zona:** 2

**Air-conditioning:** ✓

**Area:** 49+22 m²

Location

The **Village 4/5** is an ideal choice for families and couples looking for comfortable, welcoming and practical accommodation within the campsite. Designed for a relaxing stay, it offers a pleasant and functional space with everything you need to feel at home during your holiday.

The accommodation features a bright and comfortable living and dining area, together with a well-equipped kitchen including a gas hob, microwave and fridge, making everyday life during your stay easy and convenient.

The **Village 4/5** has two bedrooms: one with a double bed and another with two single beds, as well as a sofa bed in the living room, accommodating up to 5 people. It also has a fully equipped bathroom.

Outside, there is a pleasant terrace with a table and chairs, providing the perfect space for breakfast, lunch or simply relaxing and enjoying the campsite surroundings.

**Optional extras**: towels (€7 + €25 refundable deposit per set) and barbecue.
**Restrictions**: additional tents and pets are not permitted.
