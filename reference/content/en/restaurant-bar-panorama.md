# BAR PANORAMA

**Zona:** Panorama

**Schedule:** 10:00 - 23:00h

Located next to the largest pool in the campsite, Bar Panorama is the perfect place for you to enjoy a refreshing drink after a swim or at any time of the day. Relax with a drink and a snack while enjoying the lively atmosphere and panoramic views.
