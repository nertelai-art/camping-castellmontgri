# VILLAGE 6

**Capacity:** 6

**Zona:** 3

**Air-conditioning:** ✓

**Area:** 49+22 m²

Location

The **Village 6** is the perfect choice for families and groups looking for a more spacious and comfortable accommodation experience at the campsite. With its carefully designed interiors, generous layout and inviting atmosphere, it offers an ideal combination of comfort, style and practicality for a relaxing holiday.

The accommodation features a bright and welcoming living and dining area, together with a fully equipped kitchen including a gas hob, microwave and fridge. Its spacious layout provides a comfortable setting for spending time together, whether enjoying a meal or relaxing after a day at the campsite.

The **Village 6** offers three bedrooms: one with a double bed and two with two single beds each, comfortably accommodating up to 6 people. It also features a fully equipped bathroom.

One of its highlights is the outdoor terrace, furnished with a table and chairs and designed as an extension of the living space. It is the perfect place to enjoy breakfast, share a meal or unwind while making the most of the campsite surroundings.

**Optional extras**: towels (€7 + €25 refundable deposit per set) and barbecue.
**Restrictions**: additional tents and pets are not permitted.
