## Events and groups

Celebrate your ideal event at our campsite. If you have an idea and want to make it come true, don’t hesitate to contact us.

# ABOUT US

In a corner of the Costa Brava, we have the perfect combination for your event: a privileged natural setting and over 50 years of experience in the industry.

For us, your idea is the most important thing, and we’ll work together to make sure your expectations are met.

## WEDDINGS

Are you planning your wedding? Looking for an all-in-one venue?
At Camping Castell Montgrí, you can combine the ceremony, banquet, and accommodation all in the same place.
In addition, we’ll be with you throughout the entire process so that every detail reflects your personality — from the first contact to the end of the event.

## CORPORATE GROUPS

Have you heard about the benefits of team building?
If your team needs motivation, or your company simply needs a getaway to disconnect, our facilities offer the perfect solution.
With a combination of green spaces and areas for meetings and activities, along with exceptional cuisine, you’ll feel a connection with nature and your colleagues — helping to improve the workplace atmosphere.

[DOSSIER PDF](https://www.camping-castellmontgri.com/uploads/docs/dossier-corporativo-6914b969739e0898861976.pdf)

## SPORTS GROUPS

Tired of training in the same field all the time and want to practice with your team in a more natural environment?
At Camping Castell Montgrí, we have facilities for all kinds of sports: basketball, tennis, football, table tennis, hiking, swimming, water polo, yoga, and more.
If you want to organize a sports training camp and breathe in the fresh air of nature, this is the place for you.
And don’t forget — we’re just a short distance from the beach!

[DOSSIER PDF](https://www.camping-castellmontgri.com/uploads/docs/dossier-grupos-deportivos-6914b99208240332538407.pdf)

## SCOOL GROUPS

Did you know that learning can happen outside the classroom?
A getaway to nature is the perfect trip to learn, explore, share, and grow.
Whether it’s an end-of-year trip, a group retreat, or a themed excursion, our campsite is the ideal place to carry out all kinds of outdoor activities you can imagine.

[DOSSIER PDF](https://www.camping-castellmontgri.com/uploads/docs/dossier-grupos-escolares-6914b9ab35a2f800357646.pdf)

## PRIVATE GATHERINGS

At Camping Castell Montgrí, we don’t just host weddings or design stays for school, sports, or corporate groups.
If you want to celebrate a special event — a birthday, family meal, cocktail party, baby shower, bachelor or bachelorette party, or simply come with your group to enjoy a stay in nature — we’re here to bring your idea to life.
