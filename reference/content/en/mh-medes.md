# MH MEDES

**Capacity:** 6

**Zona:** 3

**Air-conditioning:** ✓

**Area:** 32 m²

Location

The MH Medes are the perfect accommodation for those seeking comfort and functionality during their camping stay. These mobile homes feature a cozy living room, a dining area, and a kitchen equipped with a gas stove, microwave, and refrigerator. They have three bedrooms: two with single beds and one with a double bed. Additionally, they have a full bathroom with a shower, sink, and a separate WC for added convenience.

The exterior is equally impressive, with a large wooden terrace that includes a table and chairs, two hammocks, and an awning to enjoy relaxing moments outdoors.

Some of these mobile homes are located in the upper part of the campsite, near the Panorama pool, while others are in the lower part, near the Oasis pool.

- **Extras available:** towels (price: €7 + €25 deposit/pack) and barbecue
- No additional tents or pets are allowed.
