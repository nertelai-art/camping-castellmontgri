# TENT PLIS PLAS

**Capacity:** 4

**Area:** 15 m²

Location

Discover classic camping with all the modern comforts! With our Plis Plas tents, we eliminate the stress of assembly, making it perfect for first-time campers to see if they truly enjoy it.

Each tent is equipped with two cabins, each with a mattress (135×190), along with a BBQ for enjoying delicious meals under the stars. An outdoor table ensures that all meals of the day are as comfortable as possible.

- **Extras available to hire:** towels (price €7 + €25 refundable deposit per pack), and Camping Gas stove (Diposit 20€)
- Additional tents or animals are not allowed.
