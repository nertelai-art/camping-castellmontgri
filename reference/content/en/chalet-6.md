# CHALET 6

**Capacity:** 6/7

**Zona:** 3

**Air-conditioning:** ✓

**Area:** 35 m²

Location

The **Chalet 6** is the perfect option for those seeking modern and elegant accommodation at the campsite. These chalets stand out for their spaciousness, sophisticated design, and attention to every detail. Each chalet features a bright living room with a contemporary design, a large dining area, and a fully equipped kitchen with a gas stove, dishwasher, microwave, and refrigerator.

With three spacious bedrooms (one with single beds, another with bunk beds, and one with a double bed), and a full bathroom, these chalets are designed to offer maximum comfort and functionality.

One of their main attractions is the impressive wooden terrace, which seamlessly integrates with the living room through large sliding glass doors. This connection creates a continuous space that enhances the sense of openness and brightness, making it ideal for enjoying both indoors and outdoors. The terrace is equipped with a table, chairs, and an awning, providing the perfect environment for outdoor relaxation.

**Available extras**: towels (price €7 + €25 refundable deposit per pack) and barbecue
**Restrictions**: no additional tents or pets allowed
