# OMBRA ICE CREAM PARLOR

**Zona:** Ombra

**Schedule:** 10:00 - 23:00h

At Ombra ice cream parlor you will discover the ideal place to indulge in a little pleasure. Located next to the café/creperie and the Ombra pool, our ice cream shop offers a wide variety of fresh and delicious ice creams, perfect for cooling off on the hottest days.
