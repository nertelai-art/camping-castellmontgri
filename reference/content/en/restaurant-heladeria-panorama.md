# PANORAMA ICE CREAM PARLOR

**Zona:** Panorama

**Schedule:** 9:00 - 23:00h

At Panorama ice cream parlor, you'll find the perfect spot to treat yourself to a little pleasure. Strategically located next to the pool, our ice cream shop offers a wide selection of fresh and delicious ice creams, ideal for cooling off on the hottest days of camping.
