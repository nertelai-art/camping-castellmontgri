# NATURE HABITAT

**Capacity:** 5

**Zona:** 2

**Area:** 25 m²

Location

Nature Habitat is the ideal choice for those who enjoy camping in tents. In addition to the usual amenities such as a equipped kitchen, these tents offer the convenience of having a sink with shower inside the unit itself.

Each Nature Habitat is designed with a room featuring a double bed, as well as another with a bunk bed and a single bed, providing comfortable space for all guests.

- **Optional extras:** towels (price €7 + €25 refundable deposit per pack) and barbecue
- Additional tents or animals are not allowed
