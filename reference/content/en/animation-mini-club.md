# MINI CLUB

**Zona:** Panorama

**Schedule:** 9:30H - 13h | 16 - 19h

At the Kids Club, every week we offer an exciting program full of fun and learning. From themed days and face painting to dream contests, mini olympics, and creative crafts, we have activities for everyone. Our goal is to ensure that children enjoy a memorable experience filled with adventures and new friendships.
