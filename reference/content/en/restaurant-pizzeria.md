# PIZZA SHOP

**Zona:** Ombra

**Schedule:** 12:00 - 23:00h

In our pizzeria, you can enjoy a wide variety of ingredients, as well as watch how our pizzas are made right before your eyes. Baked to perfection, our pizzas are a true delight at any time of the day. Whether you want to recharge with a delicious lunch before an afternoon full of fun, or end the day with a special dinner, our pizzas will leave you wanting more.
