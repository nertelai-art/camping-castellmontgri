# TENDA MONT PLA

**Capacity:** 6

**Zona:** 3

**Area:** 26 m²

Location

Mont Pla is a charming tent that offers a unique lodging experience amidst nature. Its comfortable cabins are designed to provide a cozy and relaxing stay. Accommodation options include a cabin with a double bed (140×190), a cabin with bunk beds (80×190), and another with 2 single beds (80×190), offering flexible choices for groups of friends or families.

Mont Pla tent features a well-equipped kitchen with a gas stove and a spacious fridge (85×50). To add a touch of convenience, it includes a water point with a fountain.

- **Optional extras:** towels (price €7 + €25 refundable deposit per pack) and barbecue
- Additional tents or animals are not allowed.
