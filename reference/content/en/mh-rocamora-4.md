# MH ROCAMAURA – 4

**Capacity:** 4

**Zona:** 2

**Air-conditioning:** ✓

**Area:** 26 m²

Location

The Rocamaura accommodation, ideal for up to 4 people, provides a cozy and functional space for a comfortable stay. The living room features a sofa bed, creating a relaxing atmosphere where guests can unwind. The kitchen is equipped with a gas stove and a refrigerator.

The accommodation has two bedrooms: one with a comfortable double bed and another with bunk beds, perfect for families with children or small groups. The well-designed bathroom offers a shower, a washbasin, and a WC for added convenience.

Additionally, Rocamaura boasts a charming outdoor terrace equipped with a table and chairs, providing an ideal space to enjoy outdoor meals or simply relax.

- **Available extras:** towels (price €7 + €25 refundable deposit per pack), baby kit, and barbecue
- No additional tents or pets allowed
