# MH ROCAMAURA – 6

**Capacity:** 6

**Zona:** 3

**Air-conditioning:** ✓

**Area:** 29 m²

Location

The Rocamaura accommodation, ideal for up to 6 people, is a perfect choice for families and groups. It features a master bedroom with a double bed (135×190 cm), and two additional bedrooms, each with two single beds (80×190 cm). The bathroom includes a shower, and the WC is separate for added convenience. The living room and dining area provide a cozy space to relax and share meals. The kitchen is well-equipped with a gas stove, a refrigerator (145×55 cm), an electric coffee maker, and all the necessary cooking utensils.

- **Available extras:** towels (price 7 € + 25 € de deposit per pack) baby kit, and barbecue
- No additional tents or pets allowed.
