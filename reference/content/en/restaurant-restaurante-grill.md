# RESTAURANT GRILL

**Zona:** Panorama

**Schedule:** 18:00 - 23:00h

[Menu](https://www.camping-castellmontgri.com/uploads/docs/grill-castellano-holandes-660d71ae42ea7563942019.pdf)

For lovers of grilled fish and meat, this restaurant is a true gem. With a wide variety of options, you can enjoy an exquisite dinner in the Panorama area, where the flavors of grilled fish and meat stand out in every dish. A culinary experience you must not miss.
