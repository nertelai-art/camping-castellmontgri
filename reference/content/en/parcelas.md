## PLOTS

Discover the authentic essence of camping on our spacious pitches! Immerse yourself in nature and create your own outdoor retreat. From traditional tent camping to setting up your RV, our pitches offer the freedom to choose your experience. With modern amenities and the natural backdrop of our campsite, we invite you to enjoy serenity and adventure.
