# COMMODITY PLUS PLOT

**Capacity:** 8

**Area:** 120 m²

Location

The Comodity Plus pitch, spanning 120m², offers generous and comfortable space for those seeking an unparalleled camping experience. Each pitch is equipped with access to water and electricity, with a 10-amp electrical capacity to ensure adequate lighting and the ability to charge electrical devices without inconvenience. Designed to accommodate up to 8 people, it is perfect for large groups and families who want to enjoy all the comforts while immersing themselves in the camping experience.
