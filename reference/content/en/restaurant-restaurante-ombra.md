# OMBRA RESTAURANT

**Zona:** Ombra

**Schedule:** 13:00 - 15:00h | 18:30 - 22:30h

Discover our restaurant specializing in Mediterranean cuisine in the heart of our camping site. From its cozy atmosphere to its varied menu, we offer a culinary experience that blends the best of mediterranean cuisine. Indulge in our exquisite grilled meats, artisan pizzas, flavorful rice dishes, Paella, and fresh fish. Whether you're looking for a family meal, a romantic dinner, or simply enjoying good food with friends, our Mediterranean restaurant is the perfect place to satisfy all your culinary cravings while enjoying the tranquility and natural beauty of our surroundings.
