# COMODITY PORTION

**Capacity:** 6

**Area:** 90 m²

Location

The Comodity pitch, measuring 90m², is ideal for those seeking space and comfort during their camping stay. Each pitch has access to water and electricity, with an electrical capacity of 10 amps, ensuring optimal lighting and the ability to charge electrical devices without issues. Designed to accommodate up to 6 people, they are perfect for large groups and families looking to enjoy all comforts while immersing themselves in the authentic outdoor camping experience.
