# NEW GLAMPING – 2

**Capacity:** 2

**Zona:** 1

**Area:** 12.5 m²

Location

For an intimate getaway designed for two people, our new glamping offers the perfect setting. The room features a spacious double bed at its center, creating a cozy and relaxing atmosphere. On the wooden terrace, you'll find two comfortable outdoor seats, ideal for enjoying the fresh air and surrounding views.

This environment allows for complete disconnection from everyday life and immersion into the tranquility of the natural surroundings.

- **Optional extras:** towels (price €7 + €25 refundable deposit per pack) and barbecue
- Additional tents or animals are not allowed.
