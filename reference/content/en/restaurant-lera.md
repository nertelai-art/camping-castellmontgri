# L'ERA

**Zona:** Panorama

**Schedule:** 9:00 - 23:00h

If you are looking for a relaxation space within the campsite, you have found the ideal place. At l'Era, we offer crepes, tapas, drinks, and much more. Additionally, some nights there are shows to end the day in the best possible way.
