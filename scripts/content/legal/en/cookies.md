This page explains which cookies and other data this website stores in your browser, in line with Article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 on Information Society Services (LSSI) and the Spanish Data Protection Agency's guide on the use of cookies.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember something from one page to the next or from one visit to the next. The browser's local storage (localStorage) does a similar job, and this policy treats it the same way.

## What this website uses

This website **does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies**, and stores nothing in your browser when you arrive. It only stores these two items, and only when you do something that needs them:

**NEXT_LOCALE** (first-party cookie). Created if you change language with the selector, so pages are shown in the language you chose. It lasts until you close your browser. It is a personalisation cookie you ask for, so it does not need consent.

**map-consent** (first-party local storage). Saved when you press "Load the map" in the "How to get here" section, so we do not have to ask you every time. It does not expire: it is removed when you clear this site's data in your browser.

## Third-party content

The map in the "How to get here" section comes from OpenStreetMap and does not load until you accept it. Once loaded, OpenStreetMap may use its own cookies or technologies: see its privacy policy (osmfoundation.org).

The WitBooking booking engine and the links to Google Maps, ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini open on their own websites, which have their own cookie policies.

## Administration panel

Campsite staff who edit the website sign in to a panel with a username and password. That panel uses technical cookies to keep the session open; they are needed for it to work and are never set for visitors.

## How to delete or block cookies

You can delete this website's cookies and data, or stop them being stored, in your browser settings:

**Chrome:** Settings › Privacy and security › Third-party cookies / Site data.

**Firefox:** Settings › Privacy & Security › Cookies and Site Data.

**Safari:** Settings › Privacy › Manage Website Data.

**Edge:** Settings › Cookies and site permissions.

If you block them, the website will still work, but you will have to choose the language and accept the map each time.

## Changes

If the website starts using other cookies, we will update this page and, if any of them needs your consent, we will ask for it before using it.
