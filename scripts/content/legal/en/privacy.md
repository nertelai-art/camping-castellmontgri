## What data we process and why

### When you browse the website

This website has no forms and does not ask you for any data to use it. Like any website, the server receives technical data about each visit (IP address, browser, page requested and time) so it can serve the page and protect it against attacks.

If something goes wrong while you browse, an error report is sent so we can fix it. It contains technical data about the browser and the page, but not your name, your email or any other data that identifies you directly.

**Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in keeping the website working and secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### When you make a booking

Bookings are made on the WitBooking booking engine, which processes the data on behalf of the campsite. There we ask for the data needed to manage your stay: name, contact details, dates, guests and payment.

We use this data to manage your booking and your stay, and to meet the legal obligations of accommodation providers, such as the guest register required by Spanish Royal Decree 933/2021 and tax obligations.

**Legal basis:** performance of the booking contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and compliance with legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

### When you write to us or call us

If you email or call us, we use the data you give us to reply.

**Legal basis:** your consent when contacting us and, if you ask for information in order to book, steps taken before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(a) and 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### When you load the map

In the "How to get here" section, the map comes from OpenStreetMap and only loads if you ask for it. When it loads, your browser connects to OpenStreetMap's servers, which receive your IP address.

**Legal basis:** your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given when you press "Load the map". You can withdraw it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

## Who we share data with

We do not sell your data or pass it to third parties for advertising. To run the website and bookings we work with these providers, who process data on our behalf with the safeguards required by the GDPR:

**Vercel Inc.** hosts the website. It is a US company: the transfer is covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

**Supabase Inc.** stores the website's content and images on servers in the European Union (Paris).

**Functional Software Inc. (Sentry)** receives error reports, stored on servers in the European Union (Germany).

**WitBooking** runs the booking engine.

If you load the map, the **OpenStreetMap Foundation** (United Kingdom, covered by a European Commission adequacy decision) receives your IP address.

We also share data with public authorities when the law requires it, for example with the police for the guest register.

## Links to other services

Some buttons take you to external services: Google Maps to see the route, and ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini to plan your trip. When you press them you leave this website, and whatever you write there is processed by those companies under their own privacy policies. We do not send them any of your data.

## How long we keep data

Booking data, for as long as our relationship lasts and then for the periods set by law: three years for the guest register and the periods set by tax and commercial law.

Emails you send us, for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry.

Technical visit data and error reports, for the limited time each provider keeps these logs.

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, or to receive it in a portable format, and you can withdraw any consent you have given. Write to **info@camping-castellmontgri.com** or to our postal address saying which right you want to exercise; if necessary, we will ask you to prove your identity.

If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

## Minors

Bookings can only be made by adults. We do not process data of children under 14 without the consent of their parents or guardians, except where the law requires it for the guest register.

## Security and changes

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect your data: the website only works over an encrypted connection and access to data is limited to the people who need it.

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with its date.
